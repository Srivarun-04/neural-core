Acme Innovations - Global Remote Work and Expense Policy (Effective August 1, 2026)

1. Overview
This policy outlines the guidelines for remote work and related expense reimbursements for all full-time and part-time employees at Acme Innovations.

2. Eligibility
Employees must have completed their 90-day probationary period to be eligible for the permanent remote work program. Part-time employees and contractors are eligible for hybrid work (2 days remote per week) but not fully remote work.

3. Home Office Stipend
Acme Innovations provides a one-time stipend of $850 for eligible full-time employees to set up their ergonomic home office. This stipend can be used for ergonomic chairs, standing desks, keyboards, and external monitors. It CANNOT be used for purchasing laptops or tablets, as those are provided directly by the Acme IT department.

4. Monthly Internet and Phone Reimbursement
- Fully Remote Employees: Can expense up to $75 per month for internet services and $40 per month for mobile phone plans.
- Hybrid Employees: Can expense up to $40 per month for internet. They are not eligible for mobile phone reimbursements.
Reimbursements must be submitted by the 5th of the following month via the Expensify portal.

5. Working Hours and Availability
While Acme supports flexible schedules, core collaboration hours are between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST), regardless of the employee's local time zone. Employees must be online and available on Slack and Zoom during these core hours.

6. Temporary Relocation & Blackout Dates
Employees may work from a temporary location (e.g., a vacation destination) for up to 20 days per calendar year. However, this is strictly prohibited during the end-of-year freeze, which runs from December 15th to January 5th annually, due to high-priority Q4 closing activities.

7. Compliance and Security
All remote work must be conducted over the company-provided secure VPN (AcmeShield). Accessing company databases over public Wi-Fi without the VPN is strictly prohibited and constitutes a Level 2 security violation under Section 4B of the Global Employee Handbook.
